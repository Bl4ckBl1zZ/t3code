# SnapShots

In the macOS desktop app, SnapShots capture the window you are using and attach it to
an unsent draft. Review the capture and choose Send when you are ready.

Open Settings → SnapShots to enable capture. Allow Screen Recording and, for app text
or modifier-pair shortcuts, Accessibility. macOS may ask you to restart the app; setup
resumes afterward. The setup test captures and discards one image before enabling the
shortcut.

Press both Shift keys together to capture a window, or record and save another shortcut
in Settings. Captures go to your current draft or thread. If neither is open, T3 creates
a draft in your last project. A capture stays queued if a draft cannot be created or its
attachment cannot be saved; returning to T3 retries delivery without duplicating it.

Include app text adds verified text and controls when the captured app provides them.
Use the text button on a capture to inspect exactly what was included. You can turn off
app text, sound, flash or animation independently. Reduced Motion disables capture motion.
Disabling SnapShots unregisters its shortcut.

Captures sent from desktop remain readable on your connected devices. On iPhone and iPad,
tap a capture's app label to inspect its details. Use Photos, Camera, Files or the iOS
share sheet to bring in new images; iOS does not offer desktop-style capture of other apps.
